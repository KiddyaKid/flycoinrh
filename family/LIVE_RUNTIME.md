# Continuous observation

The idle browser opens PONS Explore (`/launchpad`). A read-only presentation policy moves the visible fly cursor smoothly, scrolls, and follows an observed first-party Home/Explore navigation link when available. It never clicks a trade or launch control. Its public mode is `ambient-read-only`; those random waypoints are not neural decoder samples.

The neural worker continues independent, two-seed MaleCNS trials on captured browser frames. A birth browser takes over the video only while its fresh capture is available; when that browser finishes or becomes unavailable, the idle stream resumes. During creation, the host selects the form control and the measured decoder supplies movement and the stop gate. The actual element under the cursor must also be the selected button before clicking.

## Transaction source

The project token and the currently selected observation token are different. The observation source is explicitly shown in the UI. `live-input-pools.json` holds the verified V4 pool keys for the user-selected external test token. `node family/discover-live-pools.mjs` refreshes candidate pools from a public index and verifies their Initialize events and currency bindings against the known on-chain PoolManager.

The scanner polls with a durable SQLite cursor, waits 20 confirmation blocks, verifies every Swap against its successful transaction receipt and canonical block, and deduplicates by transaction hash plus log index. Startup history is recorded without enqueueing births. Newly confirmed events enter the bounded queue. Coverage excludes V3 and pools created since the last discovery; this is not a claim of complete GMGN feed parity. Different quote currencies retain their own units.

## Signing boundary

Observation does not grant signing authority. The local allowance remains one additional child, a cumulative 0.02 ETH budget, the exact source CA, expiry and bound birth ID. No private key enters this repository, browser or public snapshot. A paused browser attempt is not automatically retried and must never be reset into a second birth. Existing signed transactions must be reconciled by their saved hash rather than signed again.

The video port exposes only `/video.mjpeg`. Local previews connect directly; remote viewers use the published video tunnel and reconnect or fall back to the timestamped snapshot when unavailable.

The observer now reuses the persistent Chrome profile and exposes a read-only wallet bridge for the operator address. Its status is reported separately from signing. With `FAMILY_OBSERVE_ONLY=1`, the birth coordinator is disabled while browser, neural measurements and TX ingestion continue. Future authorized launch pilots attach to the same local browser on port 5192 and close only their page/client connection; they do not close the observer.
